Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who we are

Lakut Kashmir is a tour operator for Kashmir, Leh-Ladakh and Vaishno Devi. You can reach us at nadeem.lakut@gmail.com or +91 91319 90355.

What we collect

  • The details you type into our enquiry form: your name, mobile/WhatsApp number, travel month, number of travellers and the package you are interested in.
  • How you reached this page, such as the advertising campaign or search keyword (for example Google Ads click IDs and UTM tags).
  • Basic, anonymous usage data collected by Google Analytics / Google Ads and Meta when those tools are enabled, using cookies or similar technology.

How we use it

  • To contact you by phone or WhatsApp with your quote, itinerary and booking details.
  • To understand which of our advertisements are useful, so we can improve them.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the hotels, houseboats and transport partners needed to run a trip you book with us, or when the law requires it.

Where it is stored

Enquiries are stored in our private Google Sheet and email inbox, accessible only to the Lakut Kashmir team. If you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy policy also applies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 24 months so we can help you with this and future trips, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your details, or to stop contacting you, by emailing nadeem.lakut@gmail.com or messaging us on WhatsApp. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
