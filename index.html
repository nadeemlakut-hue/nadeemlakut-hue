Your days in paradise

A day-by-day taste of your trip

The Snow Magic route. Best of Kashmir adds a full day in the Doodhpathri meadows.

  1. Day 1

    Arrive in Srinagar

    Airport pick-up, then Shalimar & Nishat Bagh, Hazratbal, Pari Mahal, Shankaracharya Temple and a sunset shikara ride on Dal Lake.

  2. Day 2

    Sonmarg — Meadow of Gold

    Alpine meadows, the Sindh river and an optional pony ride or trek up to Thajiwas Glacier.

  3. Day 3

    Gulmarg — Meadow of Flowers

    Snow play, skiing in season and the famous Gulmarg Gondola, one of the highest cable cars in the world.

  4. Day 4

    Pahalgam — Valley of Shepherds

    Aru Valley, Baisaran, Chandanwari and the rushing Lidder river through pine forests.

  5. Day 5

    Fly home with memories

    Drop at Srinagar airport or railway station, with a camera roll full of Kashmir.

Are the prices per person?

Yes. All prices shown are per person, based on the room sharing in the package. Your final price depends on dates, number of travellers and hotel choice, and is confirmed in your free quote.

What is the best time to visit Kashmir?

Every season is different: December–February for snow in Gulmarg and Sonmarg, March–April for the Tulip Garden and blossoms, May–August for green meadows and pleasant weather, and October–November for golden chinar trees.

Is Kashmir safe for families and couples?

Tourists visit Kashmir in large numbers every year. Our local team travels with you through a dedicated driver, stays in touch daily and is reachable 24/7 on phone and WhatsApp.

Are flights included?

No. Packages start and end at Srinagar airport or railway station. We can suggest the best flight timings for your itinerary.

Can I customise the package?

Absolutely. Add nights, upgrade hotels, swap destinations or combine Kashmir with Leh-Ladakh or Vaishno Devi. Tell us your budget and we'll plan around it.
